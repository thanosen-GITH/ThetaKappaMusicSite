Theta Kappa Music Apps

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

The short version

Theta Kappa Music Apps are designed to work without an account and without collecting personal information. App settings and projects stay on your device. Where an App uses your local network, it does so only to provide the feature you choose to use.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the software applications provided by Theta Kappa Music that link to this page (collectively, the “Apps”), including MIDI 2.0 Controller, BRG: Beats Rates Groupings, and Rhythm Combinations. It does not apply to websites, services, or devices operated by third parties.

2. Information we collect

The Apps do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data. In particular, they do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, audio recordings, payment information, identifiers for advertising, or usage analytics.

The Apps do not require you to create an account or sign in.

3. Information stored on your device

To provide their core functionality, the Apps may store settings, layouts, projects, and related preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to restore and operate your chosen setup. It is not sent to Theta Kappa Music.

You can remove locally stored information by deleting the relevant content in an App, where available, or by removing the App from your device. Your device operating system may also provide options to clear an app’s data.

4. Local network access

MIDI 2.0 Controller uses local-network access to discover and communicate with compatible MIDI destinations on the same Wi-Fi or local network. This access is used solely to support its controller and MIDI communication features.

Information needed for that local communication, such as the availability or network address of a selected destination, is handled on your local network. It is not sent to Theta Kappa Music. Other Apps do not use local-network access unless their in-app features and permission request say otherwise.

5. Third parties, advertising, and tracking

The Apps do not include advertising, third-party analytics, or third-party tracking technologies. Theta Kappa Music does not sell or share personal data because the Apps do not collect personal data.

6. Children’s privacy

Because the Apps do not collect personal information, they do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If an App’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when those changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this page shows when this policy was last revised.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about Theta Kappa Music Apps, please use the Theta Kappa Music contact page.
